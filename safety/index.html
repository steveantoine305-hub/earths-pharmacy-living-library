How to Read a Monograph

Monographs are structured to present identification, constituents, traditional uses, dosing, safety notes, evidence tier, and references. Use the Evidence Tier and Safety sections to guide clinical decisions and cross-check with primary literature when necessary.

Red Flag Symptoms

Any of the following require urgent evaluation: difficulty breathing, facial swelling, severe abdominal pain, jaundice, persistent vomiting, uncontrolled bleeding, sudden severe headache, confusion, or loss of consciousness.

Frequently Asked Safety Questions

Can herbs interact with prescription medications?
Yes. Many botanicals impact metabolic enzymes or platelet function — always check interactions before recommending herbal therapies alongside prescriptions.
Are natural products always safe in pregnancy?
No. Pregnancy changes risking teratogenicity or altered pharmacokinetics; consult obstetric guidance and avoid known contraindicated botanicals.
How do you report a safety concern?
Contact the editorial team via the contact details in the book or the Living Evidence Library with citation details and any supporting evidence; the editorial team will review and update entries as appropriate.
