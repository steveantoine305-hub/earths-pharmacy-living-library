About The Earth’s Pharmacy

The Earth’s Pharmacy curates and preserves knowledge on medicinal plants worldwide. Our work combines historical materials, contemporary scholarship, and living evidence so practitioners and readers can access reliable, up-to-date information.

Our Mission

The Earth’s Pharmacy exists to preserve, organize, and continuously improve evidence-based knowledge about medicinal plants from around the world. We combine conservation-quality reproduction of botanical art with rigorous editorial standards and living updates so the work remains useful and accurate over time.

We prioritize clarity for clinicians and accessibility for readers while ensuring editorial transparency and traceability of sources.

Preserve • Organize • Improve

A living reference that respects tradition and centers contemporary evidence — maintained by scholars, clinicians, and conservators.

The Living Library

The Living Library is a digital companion to the printed work. Unlike a static book, it evolves: new research, corrections, safety notices, and expanded monographs are added continuously. QR codes in the Founder’s Edition and subsequent printings link directly to these live entries so readers always access the latest evidence.

Our Editorial Principles

Our editorial process is grounded in principles that guide how content is selected, reviewed, and published.

Evidence-based

We prioritize peer-reviewed studies and high-quality clinical data when available.

Transparent sourcing

All assertions link to primary sources and are documented for verification.

Safety first

Safety notices and contraindications are prominently displayed and updated as new data emerges.

Respect for traditional knowledge

We honor and cite traditional sources, working with communities to contextualize cultural practice.

Continuous updates

Entries are revised as new evidence becomes available; errata and changes are logged for transparency.

Evidence Tiers

We classify evidence to help readers interpret the strength of data behind claims and recommendations.

Tier 1 — Research-Supported (Strong human evidence)
Tier 2 — Promising (Supportive clinical or mechanistic data)
Tier 3 — Traditional Use (Documented historical or traditional use)

Contribute

We welcome submissions from researchers, physicians, herbalists, botanists, pharmacists, and institutions. Contributions may include primary research, corrections, safety reports, or source materials. Submitted evidence is reviewed by the editorial board before incorporation; contributors are credited where appropriate.

To contribute, email the editorial team or use the contact form in the Living Library (contact details and submission guidelines are provided in the Resources Center).

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the Living Library updated?
The editorial team monitors the literature and receives community submissions. Updates are curated, reviewed, and published with changelogs and references.
Can I cite the Living Library in academic work?
Yes — we provide suggested citation formats for each entry that include versioning and access dates to ensure reproducibility.
Who manages editorial decisions?
An editorial board of clinicians, botanists, and subject-matter experts oversees content selection and review.
