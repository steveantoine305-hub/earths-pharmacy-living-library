History Lives Here

The Crown of Haiti series draws from the Haitian Revolution, the reign of Henri Christophe, Sans-Souci Palace, the Citadelle, the Chasseurs-Volontaires de Saint-Domingue, Haiti’s support of liberation movements throughout the Americas, and the long struggle against foreign interference and historical erasure.

Through historical fiction, the series restores Haiti to the center of its own story—honoring its people, memory, sacrifice, sovereignty, and global influence.

About the Author

Steve Antoine

Steve Antoine is a Haitian American writer and creator of The Crown of Haiti series. His work centers Haiti’s revolutionary legacy, sovereignty, memory, leadership, and global influence through historical fiction grounded in research, cultural identity, and the lived inheritance of the Haitian people.
