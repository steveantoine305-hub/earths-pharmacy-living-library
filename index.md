# The Earth's Pharmacy

## Living Evidence Library

Welcome to the official Living Evidence Library for **The Earth's Pharmacy – Illustrated Founder's Edition**.

This website serves as the permanent companion to the printed publication.

Its purpose is to provide:

- Evidence updates
- Newly published clinical research
- Safety updates
- Regulatory changes
- Revision history
- Educational resources
- Errata and corrections

The printed edition remains the official publication of record.

The Living Evidence Library allows scientific knowledge to evolve while preserving the integrity of the published book.

## Editorial Philosophy

The Earth's Pharmacy distinguishes among:

- Traditional knowledge
- Laboratory research
- Animal studies
- Human clinical evidence

Claims unsupported by sufficient evidence are intentionally excluded.

Scientific transparency is valued above sensationalism.
