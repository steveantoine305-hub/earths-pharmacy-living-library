Public Discovery and Internal Review

Public discovery metadata and complete botanical monographs are separate layers of the Living Evidence Library.

DRAFT and internal monograph source files remain outside the public deployment artifact until the applicable verification, review, and release controls are satisfied.

Evidence Classification Principle

Evidence tiers apply to individual therapeutic claims, not to an entire plant as a single blanket rating. Different claims associated with the same botanical may carry different levels of evidence.

Safety Principle

Natural origin does not guarantee safety. Safety review is maintained separately from evidence of therapeutic benefit and may include contraindications, medication interactions, special populations, preparation risks, sourcing concerns, contamination, dose, and duration.

Living Website and Published Edition

The Living Evidence Library may continue to evolve as new evidence is reviewed. Website revisions do not alter the Founder Frozen publication edition.
