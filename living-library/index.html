How QR Codes Connect You

The Founder’s Edition and select pages carry QR codes that link directly to this Living Evidence Library. Scanning a QR code will open the most recent evidence, errata, and updates for the referenced entry — ensuring readers always access the latest research and safety guidance.

Each QR code resolves to a stable library entry and may include contextual parameters (edition, plate number, or monograph) so the content you see is tailored to the exact reference in the book.

About This Resource

This library is curated and maintained by the editorial team. It is continuously updated and cross-referenced with primary literature. If you discover a correction or have a citation to share, please contact the editorial team so entries can be reviewed and incorporated.
