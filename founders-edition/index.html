A Reference Built Differently

The Earth’s Pharmacy does not grade an entire plant as simply “proven” or “unproven.” It evaluates specific claims. A single botanical may have strong human evidence for one use, promising but incomplete evidence for another, and a documented traditional use for a third.

TIER 1

Research-Supported

Meaningful human evidence directly relevant to the stated use.

TIER 2

Promising

A credible signal exists, but evidence remains incomplete, inconsistent, indirect, preparation-specific, early, or substantially preclinical.

TIER 3

Traditional Use

Documented historical or ethnomedical use where adequate clinical evidence for that specific therapeutic claim has not been established.

Safety is evaluated separately from efficacy.

Tradition and Evidence

This work preserves African, Caribbean, Diaspora, and other botanical knowledge while refusing to blur the distinction between documented tradition, laboratory findings, animal research, human evidence, and established clinical evidence.

The Earth’s Pharmacy honors traditional knowledge without representing tradition alone as clinical proof.

Safety is Part of the Knowledge

Botanical effects and risks can depend on species, plant part, preparation, concentration, route, dose, duration, age, pregnancy or lactation, medical condition, medication use, and product identity and quality.

Natural origin does not guarantee safety.
The Earth’s Pharmacy is educational reference material. It is not individualized medical advice, diagnosis, prescribing, or treatment and is not a substitute for care from a qualified health professional.
