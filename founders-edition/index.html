About the Founder’s Edition

The Founder’s Edition is a thoughtfully produced volume celebrating the original vision behind The Earth’s Pharmacy. Each copy is printed on archival paper, bound by hand, and includes a numbered certificate of authenticity and a signed collectors plate from the editorial team.
