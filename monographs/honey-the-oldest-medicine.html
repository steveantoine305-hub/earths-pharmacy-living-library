Traditional Context

Tradition of origin &** historical use.** Documented in the Egyptian medical papyri as a wound dressing and infection preventative thousands of years before germ theory existed — one of the oldest continuously validated remedies in the human record, and central to African, Caribbean, and diaspora healing traditions alike.

Safety & Cautions

Safety Principle: Natural origin does not guarantee safety.
Contraindications:

Cautions. Never give honey to infants under one year old — risk of infant botulism, a serious, well-established safety rule with no exceptions. Otherwise very safe for children and adults. Those with diabetes should account for its sugar content.
