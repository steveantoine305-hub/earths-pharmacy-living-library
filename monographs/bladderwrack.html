Safety & Cautions

Safety Principle: Natural origin does not guarantee safety.
Contraindications:

Cautions. — This is one of the most important caution flags in the book. Bladderwrack’s iodine content is often very high and inconsistent batch to batch, and it can also concentrate heavy metals and arsenic from seawater depending on where it’s harvested. It can seriously disrupt thyroid function in both directions and should generally be avoided by anyone with a thyroid condition, and avoided in pregnancy without direct medical guidance. Source and dosage matter enormously here — this is not a casual daily tonic.
