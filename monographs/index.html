Living Library Development

The Earth’s Pharmacy Living Evidence Library is being developed as a versioned botanical reference system. Botanical records may be researched and revised internally before they become available as complete public monographs.

Evidence Review

Therapeutic claims are evaluated individually. Evidence strength is not assigned to an entire plant as a single blanket rating.

Safety Review

Contraindications, medication interactions, special populations, preparation risks, and other safety considerations are reviewed separately from efficacy.

Controlled Publication

Draft and internal monograph source files remain outside the public deployment artifact until they are explicitly approved for public release.

Claim-Level Evidence Tiers

Evidence tiers apply to individual therapeutic claims, not to an entire botanical. One plant may contain claims supported by different levels of evidence.

Tier 1 — Research-Supported: Strong human evidence from high-quality clinical research supporting the specific claim.
Tier 2 — Promising: Supportive clinical, observational, mechanistic, or other meaningful research exists, but the evidence is less conclusive.
Tier 3 — Traditional Use: The claim has documented historical or traditional use with limited modern clinical evidence.

Safety Is Evaluated Separately

Natural origin does not guarantee safety.

Botanical safety can vary according to plant identity, plant part, preparation, concentration, dose, duration, individual health conditions, pregnancy or lactation, medication use, age, sourcing, contamination, and other factors.

Safety information does not establish that a therapeutic claim is effective, and evidence of benefit does not eliminate safety considerations.

Publication Boundary

The public website and the internal botanical production system are intentionally separated. Draft source material is not part of the public GitHub Pages deployment.

Website updates to the Living Evidence Library do not alter the Founder Frozen publication edition.
