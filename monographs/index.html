Evidence Tier Explanation

The library classifies evidence by tier to help readers interpret clinical confidence: Tier 1 — strong human clinical data; Tier 2 — supportive clinical and mechanistic data; Tier 3 — preclinical or limited human data. Each monograph will display its assigned evidence tier and references.

Tier 1: High-quality randomized controlled trials and meta-analyses.
Tier 2: Controlled clinical studies, observational data, and reproducible mechanistic research.
Tier 3: Preclinical studies, traditional use reports, and limited human data.

Safety Notice

Herbal medicines can interact with pharmaceuticals and may not be safe for everyone. Always consult a qualified clinician before using botanicals therapeutically. The editorial team maintains safety notices and errata; check this section regularly for updates.
