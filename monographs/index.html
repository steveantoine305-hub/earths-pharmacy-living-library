Evidence Tier Explanation

The library classifies evidence by **Claim-Level Tiers** to help readers interpret clinical confidence. Tiers apply to individual therapeutic claims, not entire botanicals:

Tier 1 — Research-Supported: Strong human evidence from high-quality clinical trials.
Tier 2 — Promising: Supportive clinical data, observational studies, or mechanistic research.
Tier 3 — Traditional Use: Documented historical or traditional use with limited modern clinical data.

Safety Notice

Herbal medicines can interact with pharmaceuticals and may not be safe for everyone. Always consult a qualified clinician before using botanicals therapeutically. The editorial team maintains safety notices and errata; check this section regularly for updates.
